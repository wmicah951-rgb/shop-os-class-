Shop-OS · AI Estimate Agent

What you're looking at

Shop-OS is a management app for motorcycle and powersports repair shops: work orders, parts, technicians and customers in one place. This public demo shows the shop side of the app with a built-in AI agent that writes repair estimates the way a service writer would. You stay in charge.

1Open a job

Pick a work order, or create one with the vehicle and the suspected issue in the customer's words.

2Talk it through

The agent ranks likely causes and prices the repair from the shop's real parts, rates and technicians. Every tool it uses is shown. Add details and it updates the estimate.

3You approve

Saving a draft stops at a yellow approval card showing exactly what will be saved. Nothing happens until you click Approve.

Real: the parts catalog, stock, labor rates and technicians are a snapshot of the Shop-OS demo shop. Prices are calculated, never made up.
Sandbox: jobs you create and drafts you approve exist only in this browser tab. Nothing is written to Shop-OS. Customer contact details are removed.
Can't: send emails or texts, take payments, give discounts, change prices, or edit or delete records.
Limits: 12 messages per chat, 10 tool calls per message. Accountable owner: Micah (project owner, CIS 4394).
