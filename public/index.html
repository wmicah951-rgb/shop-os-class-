Shop-OS · AI Estimate Agent

What you're looking at

Shop-OS is a management app for motorcycle and powersports repair shops. This public demo lets you run a repair job end to end, with a built-in AI agent that diagnoses the problem and writes the estimate. You stay in charge of every step that matters.

1Open a job

Pick a work order, or create one with the vehicle and the suspected issue in the customer's words.

2Diagnose with the agent

It ranks likely causes and prices the repair from the shop's real parts, rates and technicians. Every tool call is shown.

3Customer approves

Edit the estimate, send it, then approve or decline it as the customer would in their portal.

4Invoice & payment

Start the work, mark it complete, issue the invoice and record a simulated payment.

Real: the parts catalog, stock, labor rates and technicians are a snapshot of the Shop-OS demo shop. Every price is calculated on the server from that data.
Sandbox: new jobs, estimates, approvals, invoices and payments live only in this browser. Nothing is written to Shop-OS, no message is sent and no money moves.
The agent can't: send emails or texts, take payments, give discounts, change prices, or approve its own actions.
Limits: 12 messages per chat, 10 tool calls per message. Accountable owner: Micah (project owner, CIS 4394).
