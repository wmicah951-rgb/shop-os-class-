What this agent can and can't do
  • Reads: work orders (vehicle, symptoms, status, no customer contact info), parts inventory, technicians, and shop rates from Shop-OS.
  • Can: look things up, build and price estimates, and request to save a draft estimate.
  • Needs you: saving a draft pauses for your Approve or Reject, with the exact arguments shown. Approved drafts go to a sandbox for this chat only. Nothing is written to the real Shop-OS database.
  • Can't: send emails or texts, take payments, give discounts, change prices, edit or delete records.
  • Privacy: chats aren't stored on the server. Don't type personal information. Accountable owner: Micah, the project owner.
